Care coordination across the public-health journey

CareBridge is positioned as a closed-loop care-coordination layer connecting existing public-health touchpoints rather than replacing them.

Care stepSystem responsibility
First contactCapture episode and support structured triage
Facility selectionMatch clinical/service need with facility capability and readiness
ReferralKeep referral active through acceptance, scheduling, arrival and outcome
Diagnostics / medicinesCoordinate availability and surface current resource information
Treatment / back-referralRecord outcome and return information to the care timeline
Follow-upAssign due date, owner and escalation for high-risk cases

Judge Q&A

Why not ABDM?

ABDM is national digital-health infrastructure. CareBridge is a care-coordination layer for the patient journey, referral closure, follow-up and readiness.

Why not eSanjeevani?

eSanjeevani provides telemedicine. CareBridge can use teleconsultation as one step inside a broader closed-loop journey.

What is actually innovative?

Capability-aware routing, closed-loop referral, resource-aware navigation, scheme awareness, offline continuity and high-risk follow-up in one integration-first workflow.

Can AI diagnose patients?

No. The intended role is decision support / triage assistance with clinical protocols, human verification, auditability and validation.

What if the internet is down?

Core data-entry and workflow actions are stored locally and queued for synchronization. Safety and conflict-handling rules must be tested.

How do you avoid adding work for ASHA/ANM/CHO?

Minimize fields, prefill known information, use role-specific screens and measure documentation time as a KPI.

How do you prove impact?

Use a pilot with baseline and post-deployment measures for referral completion, closure, waiting, diagnostics, follow-up and worker documentation burden.

Does this require new hardware?

The design targets existing Android devices where feasible. Software, integration, governance and workflow redesign are the core focus.
